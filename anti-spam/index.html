Anti-Spam Policy

How we use email

Tango Tenors is a professional music project based in Berlin, Germany. We use email for direct communication about concert proposals, bookings, performances and press information.

We contact concert organisers, venues, festivals, cultural institutions, media representatives and other professional contacts whose work appears relevant to the project. Some recipients may not have corresponded with us before. Our messages are individually addressed and may include links to our website, press materials or ticket information.

We do not operate an email newsletter or subscription marketing list. A message about a concert or booking may be an initial professional approach; we do not describe it as opt-in communication when there has been no prior subscription or correspondence.

Stop future promotional messages

If you do not want to receive further promotional or booking messages from Tango Tenors, reply to the email and ask us to stop, or write to tangotenors@gmail.com. We will record the request and exclude that address from future promotional outreach.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes the promotional emails managed by Tango Tenors from tangotenors@gmail.com. We have not identified any affiliate or third party authorized to send promotional messages on our behalf.
