Tango Tenors at the Mirror Hall of Clärchens Ballhaus: tenors Rolando Guy and Gastón Efficace bring together Argentine tango, Neapolitan song and the Italian vocal tradition. Music by Carlos Gardel and Astor Piazzolla meets Neapolitan canzoni and familiar moments from opera, carried by voice, piano, rhythm and theatrical presence. An evening of music between Buenos Aires, Naples and Berlin.
